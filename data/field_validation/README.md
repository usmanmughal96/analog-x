# Pakistan field validation campaign

**State: planned only. No site visits or new observations are claimed.**

The machine-readable station plan is `campaign_plan.json`. It defines permission and safety gates, three proposed station types for each of the four Pakistan study candidates, field measurement requirements, and the evidence-release procedure.

## Field kit

Use the browser [Field Validation Kit](../../field-validation.html) to draft planned stations and, after an authorized visit, capture actual measurements. The tool stores data in the local browser only; it does not upload observations. Export JSON/CSV, retain original photo and instrument files, and submit a controlled repository change for review.

## Site-specific field priorities

- **Chagai small playas:** playa-margin to interior transects, observed crust and sediment texture, environmental measurements, and permission-led sampling only.
- **Hamun-i-Mashkel:** basin margin, playa surface and ephemeral channel/fan transitions; explicitly separate direct observations from basin-scale hydrological inference.
- **Koh-i-Sultan:** expert-led outcrop and contact descriptions, including mapped tuff, agglomerate and andesitic units; no assumed basaltic match.
- **Hispar Glacier:** high-hazard, professional glacier-team-only work; begin with safe proximal viewpoints and avoid glacier-surface access without specialist risk controls.

## Evidence gate

A field record is not a verified result merely because it has been entered. Each station requires georeferenced measurements, original media identifiers, instrument/calibration details, permits/safety context and uncertainty notes. Independent review must identify the reviewer, date and scope before the evidence state can be raised. Review validates only the measured local property, not Moon/Mars equivalence, resources, landing safety or rover traversability.
