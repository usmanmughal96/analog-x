Scope, not hype: this is a regional scan inside ten pre-selected 30 km Earth study areas, not a global scan of Earth. A high terrain index is not a probability, geological match, new-to-science discovery, landing recommendation or rover-safety assessment.

How this regional scan works

Each 32×32 parent terrain grid is evaluated as overlapping 6×6-cell windows (about 5.55 km across at nominal 925 m support). A window is retained only when at least 70% of its elevation cells and at least 15 valid slope and local-relief samples are available. Median slope, P90 slope and P90 3×3 local relief are computed from the source-relative grids.

Terrain index: an exploratory 0–100 display index derived from the mean of three absolute feature gaps, each normalized by its interquartile range (IQR) across the scanned Earth windows. It is a relative numerical proximity measure, not an analog probability or a measure of geology, mineralogy, soil strength, dust, ice, resources or mission feasibility.

Rank stability: 500 deterministic random feature-weight draws. It reports how often a window stays in the top decile of its own parent Earth region. Overlapping windows are spatially dependent, and this sensitivity percentage is not a confidence interval.

Data support: Earth = Copernicus DEM GLO-30 DSM resampled to ~925 m; Moon = NASA LRO LOLA 80 m elevation aggregated to ~925 m; Mars = NASA MGS MOLA MEGDR 64 px/degree at roughly ~925 m. The selected 30 km Nili Patera parent grid has 50.53% observed-cell coverage and 87 complete 3×3 neighbourhoods. Only local windows passing the explicit data threshold are ranked.

Interpretation limits The regional grids are too coarse for rover-scale hazard detection or traversability. Parent regions are curated samples, not exhaustive scans. The Moon's local DEM surface is interpolated and LOLA return counts are reported as support. The Mars scan is especially constrained by MOLA coverage. No candidate is field-validated by this algorithm, and planetary differences in gravity, atmosphere, radiation, lithology, regolith, illumination and water/ice are not captured by the terrain index.
