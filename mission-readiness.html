This workbench connects ANALOG-X's global scan, ATFA pathways, Pakistan field plan, lunar terrain evidence and independent-review process. It is deliberately an evidence-gate dashboard—not a landing-site selector, rover-safety certificate or claim that fieldwork has happened.

Safety and scientific boundary: no Earth site or lunar/Mars terrain profile is declared safe here. A regional DEM, a shortlist rank or a browser checklist cannot establish rover trafficability or mission suitability. Field deployment still requires permissions, local risk assessment and competent human approval.

05 · Rover / mission-suitability gate

The repository can screen regional terrain and document missing evidence. It cannot certify safe traverse, landing or base suitability.

01
High-resolution, co-registered hazard terrainEarth GLO-30 is aggregated to ~925 m for the current comparison. The lunar Site04 5 m product is useful topographic context, but it does not cover every operational hazard.
02
Vehicle-specific engineering envelopeNo rover mass, wheel geometry, clearance, traction, braking, power or validated slope/step limits are specified in this release.
03
Surface material and geotechnical propertiesNo measured bearing strength, cohesion, sinkage, wheel slip, grain-size distribution or regolith mechanical model for the selected route.
04
Instrumented route trials and independent engineering reviewNo rover/UGV traverse results, blind prediction comparison, failure log or engineering sign-off is present.
05
Mission-environment constraintsIllumination / PSR geometry, thermal cycling, radiation, dust charging, communications and mission-date SPICE analysis are not integrated as an operational clearance package.
Current state: regional terrain screening only — operational suitability not established. Do not translate slope cutoffs into a “safe” label.
