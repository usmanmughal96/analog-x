The deck is limited to **seven slides** and is written in English for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project presentation. It includes the four Pakistan study windows, current Earth/Moon/Mars evidence, the four-site Mission Rehearsal Lab, ATFA, offline evidence tools, global ranking-release status and the remaining independent-validation work.

## Navigation and viewing

Use the on-screen Previous / Next controls or the left/right arrow keys. On desktop, an individual slide can scroll vertically when its content exceeds the 16:9 stage; on mobile, slides flow as full-height pages. Use **Print / PDF** to create a printable copy from the browser.

## Evidence language

The presentation separates ranking-file integrity from scientific validation. The Moon and Mars shortlists pass schema, ordering and rank-integrity checks; external review labels have not been returned and the Pakistan field-observation ledger is empty. No calibrated accuracy, field validation, rover trial or operational safety certification is claimed.

The NASA Space Apps Challenge mark on the title slide links to the official brand guidance. It identifies the project as a participant-built challenge submission, not as an award, NASA product or NASA endorsement.
