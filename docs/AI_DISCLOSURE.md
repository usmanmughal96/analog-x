# Super Intelligence / AI-use disclosure

The project team used ChatGPT as an AI-assisted tool during early brainstorming, project refinement, drafting, coding assistance, documentation and preparation of presentation materials. The initial concept development included AI-assisted brainstorming; this is disclosed rather than represented as entirely unaided ideation.

The team must ensure that all scientific claims, data choices, code, maps and interpretations are independently reviewed and understood by the registered participant. AI output is not treated as scientific evidence. All numerical findings must be calculated from identified source data and independently checked.

The team reports that its Local Lead approved proceeding after being informed. This disclosure does not replace any required organizer interpretation of the competition rules. All AI-generated or AI-assisted content must be labelled or acknowledged in the manner required by the official challenge guidance.
