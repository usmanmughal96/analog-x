# Submission positioning

## Short description

ANALOG-X is a Pakistan-first planetary analog evidence atlas. It builds reproducible, source-traceable dossiers for Koh-i-Sultan's volcanic terrain and Hispar Glacier's high-altitude cryosphere, combining NASA POWER climatology, Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A observations and documented local/partner-agency evidence to plan specific Earth field-rehearsal workflows for Moon/Mars exploration.

## What is the actual product?

- A public mission-evidence dashboard with NASA GIBS imagery.
- A reproducible NASA POWER snapshot pipeline.
- Public Sentinel-2 L2A scene discovery through Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC.
- A 20 m common-grid spectral-context processor for two Pakistan AOIs.
- Site-specific evidence profiles with provenance and quality/masking metrics.
- A field-experiment card that identifies tasks, equipment and non-transferable planetary conditions.
- A partner-data register showing what is accessible, what requires authentication and what has not been ingested.

## What is not claimed

- We do not claim that Koh-i-Sultan is geologically identical to Syrtis Major.
- We do not claim Hispar Glacier reproduces the lunar south pole or permanently shadowed regions.
- We do not claim Sentinel-2 ratios identify minerals.
- We do not claim NDSI alone produces a verified glacier outline.
- We do not claim NDMA or SUPARCO lack monitoring systems.
- We do not claim to have downloaded protected EvK2CNR climate data or unpublished SUPARCO/PRSS-1 scenes.
- We do not claim a globally unique or first-ever analog ranker.

## Judge-facing novelty

The differentiated work is the Pakistan-focused, reproducible evidence package: actual orbital scene selection, scale-controlled spectral screening, NASA climate baselines, traceable source/access status and task-specific field validation gaps for two contrasting candidate environments. The novelty should be demonstrated by the data outputs, not asserted as a slogan.
