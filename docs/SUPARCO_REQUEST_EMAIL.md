# SUPARCO Space4Climate data request

To: space4climate@suparco.gov.pk
Subject: Request for Space4Climate Earth-observation products and metadata for Chagai–Koh-i-Sultan research prototype

Dear Space4Climate Team,

We are developing ANALOG-X, an independent NASA Space Apps Challenge research prototype examining whether selected aspects of the Koh-i-Sultan volcanic field in Chagai, Balochistan, can support terrestrial rehearsal of field-geology and robotic geological reconnaissance workflows relevant to Mars exploration.

Could you please advise whether Space4Climate or SUPARCO can provide any Chagai-area products relevant to land cover/land degradation, land-surface temperature, aerosol observations, or hyperspectral/surface characterization? For any available product, we would appreciate the spatial and temporal coverage, spatial resolution, processing level, data format, access/download method, validation information, citation requirements and reuse licence.

We will not claim to have integrated a product unless it is received, its coverage is confirmed, and its terms permit the intended use. Any acknowledgement will follow SUPARCO's instructions.
