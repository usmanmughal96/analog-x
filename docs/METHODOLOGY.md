# ANALOG-X Methodology

## Workflow
1. Choose a planetary reference: Syrtis Major, Jezero or the lunar south-polar/Shackleton context.
2. Choose a field workflow to rehearse.
3. Select built-in Earth candidates or add custom coordinates.
4. Query NASA POWER Climatology at each Earth coordinate.
5. Calculate a task-specific relative Field-Campaign Index across successful candidates.
6. Suggest field months from monthly climatology.
7. Generate an exportable field-test card with protocol, equipment, data, limitations and source links.

## Evidence separation
- **Measured:** NASA POWER Earth meteorology and solar climatology.
- **Curated geology:** Smithsonian/USGS source descriptions and map links. These are not inferred from weather.
- **Planetary references:** NASA MOLA, LRO Diviner/LOLA and USGS Syrtis Major MC-13. The browser app does not claim to process raw planetary rasters.
- **Potential local data:** SUPARCO Space4Climate. No product is claimed as integrated until its actual data, spatial coverage, access and reuse terms are verified.

See `ALGORITHM.md` for exact formulas.
