An offline-first field capture kit for Pakistan candidate areas. Record station coordinates, observed geology/geomorphology, calibrated measurements, photos, sample provenance, access and safety notes, and independent review. Drafts remain in this browser until you export them.

Evidence integrity: only enter observations actually collected in the field. This tool does not create field validation by itself. Check access permissions, protected-area restrictions, weather, local security and safety before fieldwork. Never collect or transport samples without applicable permission.
